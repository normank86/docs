# GridGrab Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 20, 2026

GridGrab ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use GridGrab.

This page applies to **GridGrab** only. For RefTracker privacy details, see [/privacy/](/privacy/).

---

## Information We Collect

GridGrab does not require account creation.

The app may process or store the following:

- Gameplay progress and settings stored on your device
- Basic device and app interaction data used for app functionality and ads

---

## Advertising (AdMob)

GridGrab uses **Google AdMob** to display in-app advertisements.

AdMob and its partners may collect and use certain data to provide and personalize ads, including:

- Device identifiers and approximate location signals
- Ad interaction data (views, taps, and performance)
- Diagnostic and performance information

For more details, review:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

If you are in a region that requires consent, the app may present a consent prompt where available.

---

## Analytics and Diagnostics

GridGrab may use crash reporting and diagnostics to improve reliability and gameplay stability. This information is used to troubleshoot issues and improve the app experience.

---

## Data Sharing

GridGrab does not sell your personal information.

Data may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate app features such as advertising and diagnostics, or when required by law.

---

## Children's Privacy

GridGrab is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
