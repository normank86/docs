# RefTracker and GridGrab

Two iOS apps from the same team, built for very different moments:

- RefTracker keeps soccer referees focused and organized on match day.
- GridGrab delivers fast, tactical puzzle gameplay on iPhone and iPad.

## RefTracker {#reftracker}

**RefTracker** is a match management app for soccer referees who need reliable tools without extra friction.

Track goals, cards, match time, and key events quickly from your iPhone and Apple Watch.

### RefTracker Highlights

- Match event tracking built for active officiating
- Fast actions for goals, cautions, send-offs, and notes
- Apple Watch support for on-field control
- Clean workflows designed for real game pace

For help, troubleshooting, or feature requests, visit [/support/](/support/).

---

## GridGrab {#gridgrab}

**GridGrab** is a new iOS puzzle game for iPhone and iPad focused on quick strategy and satisfying pattern play.

### GridGrab Highlights

- Built for short sessions or longer score-chasing runs
- Designed for both phone and tablet play styles
- Focused gameplay loop that is easy to learn and hard to master

GridGrab uses ads in the app through AdMob.

- Privacy details for GridGrab are available at [/gridgrab/privacy/](/gridgrab/privacy/).
- Support for GridGrab uses the same email: [support@reftracker.app](mailto:support@reftracker.app)
